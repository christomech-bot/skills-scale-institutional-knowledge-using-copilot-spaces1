# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsors / Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide strategic sponsorship, funding support, and executive-level decisions for an initiative.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm alignment with organizational strategy and authorize major decision gates
- Help secure resources and remove organization-level impediments
- Approve significant scope, priority, timeline, or risk trade-offs
- Escalate business-impacting blockers when team-level resolution is insufficient

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Project Manager on governance, escalations, and status visibility
- Partner with the Product Manager on outcomes, priority, and scope decisions
- Receive recommendations from Developers, QA/Testing, and Stakeholders through the PM and Product Manager

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business needs and workflows into clear requirements, process impacts, and acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document requirements and business rules
- Map current and future workflows and identify process impacts
- Clarify acceptance criteria and unresolved questions
- Identify affected stakeholders, dependencies, and assumptions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to refine problems, scope, and backlog items
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to make requirements testable
- Work with the Project Manager to surface dependencies and coordinate stakeholder input

---

## UX / Service Designers

### Role Summary
UX and Service Designers represent user needs and shape usable, accessible experiences across products and services.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct or coordinate user research and usability validation
- Create journeys, workflows, prototypes, and experience requirements
- Identify accessibility and usability risks early
- Validate that proposed solutions address user needs

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager on discovery, prioritization, and success measures
- Collaborate with Developers and QA/Testing to make designs feasible and verifiable
- Coordinate with Stakeholders and Customer/Support Representatives to incorporate real-world feedback

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction, design quality, non-functional requirements, and technical risk management.

### Responsibilities
- Define or review technical approaches and system boundaries
- Identify technical dependencies, constraints, and risks
- Establish non-functional requirements for performance, reliability, and maintainability
- Support design reviews and technical decision records

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Guide Developers while preserving collaborative implementation ownership
- Partner with the Product Manager and Project Manager on trade-offs, estimates, and risks
- Coordinate with QA/Testing, Security/Privacy, and Release/Operations representatives on quality and readiness

---

## Security / Privacy Partners

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Partners advise teams on protecting information, managing threats, and meeting applicable security, privacy, and compliance expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Review threats, sensitive data flows, and proposed mitigations
- Advise on testing, access controls, incident readiness, and exceptions
- Confirm that security and privacy risks are tracked and escalated appropriately

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with the Technical Lead and Developers to design and implement controls
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on security and privacy validation
- Advise the Project Manager on risk status and the Product Manager on customer or business trade-offs
- Partner with Release/Operations on deployment and incident readiness

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads ensure that instrumentation, reporting, and analysis support product decisions and measurable outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Translate success metrics into measurement and instrumentation requirements
- Define data quality, reporting, and analysis needs
- Validate that metrics can be collected and interpreted responsibly
- Analyze outcomes and communicate evidence for iteration

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to define success measures and evaluate impact
- Work with Developers on instrumentation and with QA/Testing on data validation
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on reporting cadence and with Stakeholders on results

---

## Release / Operations / SRE Representatives

### Role Summary
Release, Operations, and Site Reliability representatives ensure that solutions are deployable, observable, reliable, and supportable in production.

### Responsibilities
- Review deployment plans, operational dependencies, and rollback strategies
- Define or validate observability, alerting, capacity, and reliability requirements
- Confirm incident response and support readiness before release
- Participate in post-release verification and operational learning

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with the Technical Lead and Developers on deployability and system reliability
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on smoke tests and release validation
- Partner with the Project Manager on release timing and risks
- Provide operational readiness input to the Product Manager and Stakeholders

---

## Customer Success / Support Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Representatives bring customer impact, service readiness, and frontline feedback into delivery decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Communicate customer needs, recurring issues, and service impacts
- Review support workflows, documentation, and readiness plans
- Help prepare customer communications and escalation guidance
- Monitor feedback after release and identify follow-up needs

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager and UX/Service Designer on customer needs and usability
- Work with the Project Manager on stakeholder communications and readiness tracking
- Coordinate with QA/Testing and Release/Operations on support scenarios and known issues
- Provide feedback to Developers through agreed issue and escalation channels

---

## Change / Adoption Leads

### Role Summary
Change and Adoption Leads help people understand, adopt, and sustain new products, services, or processes.

### Responsibilities
- Identify affected audiences and adoption risks
- Plan training, enablement, communications, and rollout support
- Define adoption measures and collect feedback after release
- Coordinate readiness activities and transition ownership to operational teams

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with the Project Manager on readiness plans, milestones, and communications
- Partner with the Product Manager on desired behavior and adoption outcomes
- Coordinate with Customer/Support Representatives and Stakeholders on training and announcements
- Feed adoption results and improvement opportunities into retrospectives and future planning

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Assign the relevant personas during initiation and planning, then confirm their handoffs and decision rights in the project plan, risk register, release plan, and retrospective actions.
